The season squeeze

Demand peaks for a few months. Hiring for them never works.

Every year, from festivals through the wedding months, orders arrive faster than a boutique's team can finish them. Hiring full-time karigars for just 2–4 months is hard, slow and risky — so most boutiques do the sensible thing and turn orders away.

That turned-away order is the real cost. It's revenue lost, and a client who may not come back.

The fix isn't more headcount. It's using the headcount you have on the work only they can do.

Per hour

Questions boutiques ask

Straight answers.

What if the finish isn't up to my standard?

That's what the trial piece is for. You check it against your own standard before giving me anything more. If a piece needs correcting, I fix it.

Do I have to provide the materials?

Yes. You supply the stones, pearls, mirrors, sequins, beads and tools, so there's nothing for me to buy and your costing stays exactly as you planned it.

Will you work with my clients directly?

No. I work only through you. Your clients, your designs and your label stay entirely yours.

I only need help for a few weeks. Is that okay?

That's exactly who this is for. There's no long-term commitment. Use me for the rush, then stop when the season does.

Where is the work done?

Across Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula. We can decide at the start whether I work at your boutique or collect and deliver pieces, whichever suits your workflow.

How do I get started?

Message me on WhatsApp with a photo of the work. We'll fix a trial piece and take it from there.
